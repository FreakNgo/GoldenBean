Two friends, one corner, a lot of coffee.

Cafe Golden Bean began as a college dream and turned into a real cafe on the bus route in Rajarampuri. The wall of sticky notes by the window is where our guests leave their thoughts, wishes and doodles.

Everything on the menu is made to be shared with friends: cheesy pizzas, loaded wraps, crunchy fries and drinks that taste like a treat.
